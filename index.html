BIS Aug

Best-in-slot augments for your EverQuest gear, from your inventory file.

Load your inventory and BIS Aug checks every aug socket on the gear you're wearing:

  • Optimizes your augs with a weighting system: each stat (AC, HP, heroic stats, mana and more) gets a weight, and every aug is scored by those weights. You set the weights, and caps so stats you've maxed stop counting. Weapon sockets rank by weapon damage, or for casters and healers by sympathetic procs.
  • Respects lore: a lore aug is only used once, and it tells you when moving an aug you already wear to another slot is worth more.
  • Tells you what to farm: upgrades you already own in your bags or bank first, then the ones to go get, with links showing where each one drops.
  • Lists your spell augs (type 3): the highest spell in each line at your level, and which augs you have or are missing.

Results only include augs your class can use, at or below your level, from the expansion you pick.

1Load your inventory file

Drop your inventory file here

In game, type /outputfile inventory. The file is saved in your EverQuest folder as Name_server-Inventory.txt.

Your file is read in your browser and kept only in this browser. It is never uploaded.

2Set your character

Fill out your stat weights before trusting any suggestion

There are no presets. Until you add stats under Stat weights, nothing gets suggested.

A weight is an exchange rate. Pick the stat you care about most and make it worth 1. Every other stat is worth however many of those one point of it gives you.

  • Wizard, level 85: each point of HInt gives about 24 mana, so Mana is worth 1 and HInt is worth 24.
  • Cleric, level 85: the same idea with Wisdom. HWis gives Wisdom casters about as much mana as HInt gives a Wizard, so Mana 1 and HWis about 24.
  • Tank: make HP worth 1 and HSta worth the HP one point gives you. To find it, swap in an aug with a known amount of HSta and see how much your max HP changes.
  • Capped stats: Mana Regen worth 50 with your cap of 103 is chased until you reach 103, then ignored so other stats take over.
  • Tiebreakers: add a stat worth 0, like Mana Regen after HInt and Mana. It only decides between augs that otherwise score the same. Lower rows break the ties left over.

The numbers change with level and class, so check yours in game.

Stat weights
Weapon sockets rank by weapon damage, or by sympathetic procs when that's on.

Add a row for each stat you care about. Stats you don't add are ignored. A stat worth 0 is a tiebreaker. Caps are optional: type the cap from your in-game stats window, and points past it count as 0.

Ignored augs

Augs you never want suggested, like a bad camp or a quest you won't do. Ones you already own still count. This list is shared by all your characters.
