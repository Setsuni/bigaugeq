BIS Aug

Best-in-slot augments for your EverQuest gear, from your inventory file.

Load your inventory and BIS Aug checks every aug socket on the gear you're wearing:

  • Optimizes your augs with a weighting system: each stat (AC, HP, heroic stats, mana and more) gets a weight, and every aug is scored by those weights. Pick a role preset or set your own. Weapon sockets rank by weapon damage, or for casters and healers by sympathetic procs.
  • Respects lore: a lore aug is only used once, and it tells you when moving an aug you already wear to another slot is worth more.
  • Tells you what to farm: upgrades you already own in your bags or bank first, then the ones to go get, with links showing where each one drops.
  • Lists your spell augs (type 3): the highest spell in each line at your level, and which augs you have or are missing.

Results only include augs your class can use, at or below your level, from the expansion you pick.
