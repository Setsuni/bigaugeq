BIS Aug

Best-in-slot augments for your EverQuest gear, from your inventory file.

Load your inventory and BIS Aug checks every aug socket on the gear you're wearing:

  • Optimizes your augs with a weighting system: each stat (AC, HP, heroic stats, mana and more) and each focus effect (spell damage, haste, mana cost and more) gets a weight, and every aug is scored by those weights. Pick a role preset or set your own. Weapon sockets rank by weapon damage.
  • Respects lore: a lore aug is only used once, and it tells you when moving an aug you already wear to another slot is worth more.
  • Tells you what to farm: upgrades you already own in your bags or bank first, then the ones to go get, with links showing where each one drops.
  • Lists your spell augs (type 3): the highest spell in each line at your level, and which augs you have or are missing.

Results only include augs your class can use, at or below your level, from the era you pick.

Drop your inventory file here or

In game, type /outputfile inventory. The file is saved in your EverQuest folder as Name_server-Inventory.txt.

Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.

Stat weights
Weapon sockets rank by weapon damage.

Focus weights: points per 1% of a focus effect. A focus only counts once per type, only when it beats the focus your gear already gives, and less when its level limit is below yours.
